**Nature Communications · 2026**  
Xiao Wang, Quancheng Du, Qiong Wu, Xiaofeng Jia, Liang Lin, Ljubo Vlacic, Changyin Sun, and Fei-Yue Wang

8(4), pp. 2888–2898 (2023)

<td width="62%" valign="top">
<p><strong>Driving styles · Multimodal planning · Intersection safety</strong></p>
<p>Planning at intersections requires reasoning about drivers who may respond differently to the same situation. This two-stage method combines mixture-of-experts learning and Transformer-based interaction modeling to predict surrounding trajectories and generate candidate plans. A safety-balanced value function then selects among those plans. The study examines how driving-style awareness can support safer and more context-sensitive intersection behavior.</p>

Xiao Wang, Ke Tang, Xingyuan Dai, Jintao Xu, Quancheng Du, Rui Ai, Yuxiao Wang, and Weihao Gu  
9(2), pp. 3220–3231 (2024); first published online in 2023

<table>
<tr>
<td width="38%" valign="top">
<a href="https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/stamp/stamp.jsp?tp=&amp;arnumber=10337798"><img src="assets/s4tp.png" width="360" alt="S⁴TP architecture coupling social-aware trajectory prediction with a social-aware driving risk field."></a>
<br><sub>Figure 1. S⁴TP architecture coupling social-aware trajectory prediction with a social-aware driving risk field. Source: the linked article.</sub>

<td width="62%" valign="top">
<p><strong>Interactive prediction · Risk fields · Online trajectory optimization</strong></p>
<p>S⁴TP couples social-aware trajectory prediction with a social-aware driving risk field. Predictions account for the autonomous vehicle’s planned motion, while risk maps describe interactions with surrounding human-driven vehicles. Online optimization uses these signals to seek lower-risk trajectories. Tests in SMARTS cover unprotected left turns, merging, cruising, and overtaking, with claims scoped to those evaluated scenarios.</p>
