# Consequence-Aware Decision Making for Autonomous Robotics

**Research on safe, interpretable, and socially responsible autonomous systems.**

This project brings together work on how autonomous systems anticipate behavior, assess the consequences of their decisions, and interact with people. The collection connects trajectory prediction and planning with parallel intelligence and responsibility-sensitive governance.

## Research themes

- **Understanding interactions:** interpretable trajectory prediction and driving-style awareness.
- **Assessing consequences:** safety margins, driving risk, comfort, and social compliance.
- **Studying systems before deployment:** parallel driving, foundation intelligence, and computational experiments.
- **Designing responsible institutions:** human–autonomous organizations and decentralized science.
